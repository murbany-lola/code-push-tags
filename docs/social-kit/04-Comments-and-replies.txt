REPLY: “How do I get it?”

Start here: https://github.com/murbany-lola/code-push-tags/blob/main/INSTALL.md
Pick your sounds and repos in the app 🎛️

REPLY: “Does everyone hear this?”

Only your own Mac. Your teammates have to opt into this nonsense themselves.

REPLY: “Can I turn it off?”

One global mute, including in the mini player. Even i knew there needed to be an emergency exit.

REPLY: “Can I choose the sounds?”

Yep. Push, merge, CI failure, and PR comments have separate choices. Search a producer, expand their stack, and double-click a sound to preview it. DJ decks or boombox. Your deeply unnecessary choice.

REPLY: “Does it come with sounds?”

17 clips are in the app, including 9 coding parodies. They work offline. Refresh Library gets new shared clips when they are published.

REPLY: “Can I add my own sounds?”

You can fork it and add PCM WAV files to the manifest. There is no local import button yet. Source steps: https://github.com/murbany-lola/code-push-tags/blob/main/FORKING.md

REPLY: “What’s the little LED thing?”

A tiny floating player. It shows the clip and producer, animates pixel bars while a sound plays, and has a global mute. It does not grab focus when a sound starts.

REPLY: “Does it work with private repos?”

It uses your own GitHub access. Pick the repos or organizations you want it to follow in the app.

REPLY: “Does it work on Windows / Intel?”

Apple Silicon Mac preview for now. My DJ career has a limited tour schedule.

REPLY: “Why did you make this?”

Because git was missing a mixtape era and apparently that was my problem to solve.

REPLY: “Can I fork it?”

Please do. The original platform code is MIT. Audio is separate from that code license. Fork it. Make it worse.

REPLY: “Is the audio lossless?”

The app uses PCM WAV files. Some sources were MP3s, so the conversion does not make them lossless originals. The slogan remains extremely committed to the bit.
